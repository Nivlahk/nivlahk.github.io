NEFS: a featural script designer for the IPA

NEFS (New Engineered Featural Script) is a featural script designer for the International Phonetic Alphabet. Every phonetic feature gets its own stroke, every sound's glyph is built from the strokes of its features, and you can redraw any of them and download the result as a working font.

Open NEFS → FAQ

What a featural script is

In most alphabets, the shape of a letter tells you nothing about its sound. In a featural script, it does: each part of a glyph stands for one phonetic feature, so the glyph spells out how the sound is made. Korean Hangul is the best-known example; its consonant shapes echo the position of the tongue and lips.

NEFS starts from Tessera, a featural script for the IPA. In Tessera, every glyph is exactly the combination of its features' radicals, and that holds for all 105 symbols it covers. Sounds that differ by one feature look one stroke apart.

The feature slots

A glyph is assembled from up to twelve slots. You choose a radical for each value in each slot:

SlotValues (examples)
Placebilabial, labiodental, dental, alveolar, post-alveolar, palatal, velar, uvular, pharyngeal, epiglottal, glottal
Mannerplosive, nasal, trill, flap, fricative, approximant
Voicingvoiced, voiceless
Articulator, lateral, airstreamapical (tongue tip), lateral, glottalized ingressive (implosives)
Backnessfront, central, back
Heightclose, close-mid, open-mid, open
Rounding, tenseness, r-colour, schwarounded, lax, rhotacized, the neutral vowel

An empty radical is the default for its slot. In Tessera, plosive and voiced are blank, so the most common consonants carry the fewest strokes. Near-close and near-open vowels aren't heights of their own: they are a height plus the lax radical.

How to design a script with NEFS

  1. Choose a radical for every slot. Click a value and draw its radical with the pen, line, and dot tools (snapping keeps lines and dots on the grid), or start from one of Tessera's strokes. The editor shows every sound your change affects.
  2. Give individual sounds their own symbol. A special symbol replaces the built-up glyph for one sound. It can also cover a sequence such as tʃ or aɪ, which then draws as a single square wherever it's typed.
  3. Check every sound. The results grid shows each sound in your script. A red dot marks sounds that already have a special symbol.
  4. Write with it. Type IPA and see it in your script, with IPA labels on or off.
  5. Keep it. Your script saves itself in your browser. Export it to a file to back it up or move it to another device, or import one.

Turning your script into a font

NEFS downloads your script as an installable .ttf font, so you can type in it in any app. Diacritic marks such as ʰ and ̃ overlay the sound before them, and sequence symbols such as tʃ are ligatures, so they join in apps that support ligatures, which most do.

You can also download what you've written as SVG or PNG. Two styles exist only on the page, not in the font: bold for stress, and a dashed stroke for unreleased stops.

Why make a featural script for the IPA

Tessera began as a phonetics teaching tool. When a learner meets a new sound, the most useful thing to see is how it differs from a sound they already make. A featural glyph shows that difference directly: one stroke changes, and that stroke names the feature.

NEFS opens that system up. Conlangers can build a featural script for their own language's sounds, and teachers can draw radicals that match the way they already explain articulation.

Frequently asked questions

What is a featural writing system?
A script in which parts of each glyph stand for phonetic features, such as where in the mouth a sound is made or whether it is voiced. Korean Hangul is the best-known example.
How do I design a featural script for my conlang?
In NEFS, draw a radical for each feature value, add special symbols for any sounds or sequences that need their own shape, check the full sound grid, then export your script.
Can I turn my script into a font?
Yes. NEFS downloads your script as a .ttf font you can install and type with. Diacritics overlay the preceding sound and sequences such as tʃ are ligatures.
Which sounds does NEFS cover?
It starts from Tessera, which covers 105 IPA symbols, plus diacritic marks. Special symbols let you add sequences such as tʃ or aɪ as single glyphs.
Is my script saved?
It saves automatically in your browser. Use Export script to keep a copy in a file or move it to another device.
Is NEFS free?
Yes. It runs in the browser with no install or account.
