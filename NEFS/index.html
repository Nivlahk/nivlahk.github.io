Build your own script

I · The slots

Choose a radical for every slot

Click a value to draw its radical, or start from one of Tessera's strokes. An empty radical is the default for its slot, the way plosive and voiced are blank in Tessera.

II · Special symbols

Give a sound its own symbol

A special symbol replaces the built-up glyph for one sound. It can also be a sequence, like tʃ or aɪ, which then draws as one square wherever it's typed.

III · The result

Every sound in your script

Click any sound to draw a special symbol for it. A red dot marks sounds that already have one.

V · Keep it

Save, share, install

Your script saves itself in this browser. Export it to keep a copy or move it to another device, and download it as a font to type it in any app.

In the font, marks like ʰ and ̃ overlay the sound before them, and sequence symbols like tʃ are ligatures, so they join in apps that support ligatures (most do). Stress bolding and the dashed "no release" style only exist on this page.
