Privacy Policy

Syntax Tree Sandbox · Effective October 28, 2026

In short: Syntax Tree Sandbox has no server that receives your data. What you save goes to a file in your own Google Drive, and Nivlahk cannot see it.

Who runs Syntax Tree Sandbox

Syntax Tree Sandbox is made and operated by Nivlahk (“we”). You can reach us at yefoolfish@gmail.com.

What Syntax Tree Sandbox accesses in your Google account

If you choose to connect Google Drive, Syntax Tree Sandbox asks Google for one permission: see, edit, create and delete only the specific Google Drive files you use with this app (the drive.file scope). With it, Syntax Tree Sandbox can only reach files that it created in your Drive. It cannot see, list or open any of your other files.

Syntax Tree Sandbox does not request your name, email address, contacts or profile. Google gives the app a temporary access token, which is kept only in your browser tab's memory and is gone when you close the tab.

How your data is used

  • Your work is saved as a database file in your own Google Drive, so you can reopen it later or on another device.
  • All reading, writing and processing happens inside your browser. Requests go directly from your browser to Google.
  • If you turn on “Readable copies,” Syntax Tree Sandbox also keeps plain JSON copies of your data in a folder in your Drive, so you can analyze it with tools of your choice. You can turn this off at any time; the copies are then moved to your Drive trash.

Encryption

You can choose to encrypt your database with a passphrase. Encryption happens in your browser (AES-256-GCM, with the key derived from your passphrase). Your passphrase never leaves your device and is not stored by Syntax Tree Sandbox. Neither Google nor we can read an encrypted file, and nobody can recover it if the passphrase is lost. If you choose a plain file instead, it is stored like any other file in your Drive.

What we receive and share

We do not receive, store, sell or share your Google user data or anything you create in Syntax Tree Sandbox. There are no ads and no tracking cookies. Syntax Tree Sandbox does not transfer Google user data to anyone, and does not use it for advertising or to train AI models.

The website is served by a web host, which, like any web server, may record standard request logs such as IP addresses and the pages requested.

To display the app, this page also loads files from: jsDelivr (cdn.jsdelivr.net); Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com). Like any website, those services receive your IP address and basic browser information when the files are requested. Syntax Tree Sandbox sends them none of your app data.

Syntax Tree Sandbox's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Storage and deletion

Your data stays in your Google Drive for as long as you keep it there. To delete it, delete the file (and the readable-copies folder, if you made one) from your Drive. To remove Syntax Tree Sandbox's access entirely, visit myaccount.google.com/permissions. Your browser may keep app settings in its local storage, which you can clear in your browser settings.

Children

Syntax Tree Sandbox is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. Because we receive no user data, we hold none to delete.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update it on this page and change the effective date above.
