Runs entirely in your browser; nothing you enter is sent anywhere. Results support clinical judgement and are not a diagnosis. Built on the NEFS v4 phoneme encoding: every sound is one byte whose high nibble is its place and whose low nibble is its manner and voicing, so a substitution is read directly as the features that changed. Severity bands for percent consonants correct follow Shriberg and Kwiatkowski (1982). Weak syllable deletion uses the stress marks ˈ and ˌ when the target has them; for unmarked words it falls back to counting vowels, and the word is labelled as an estimate.
