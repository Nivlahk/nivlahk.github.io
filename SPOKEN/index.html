Text methods

Put a dot after any text and name what you want done. They chain left to right: name.trim().title().replace(" ", "_"). A text method never changes the original; it gives back new text.

MethodDoesExample

title() / capitalize()Capitalizes every word / just the first letter"ada lovelace".title() → "Ada Lovelace"
trim()Removes spaces from both ends" hi ".trim() → "hi"
split(sep)Cuts text into a list"a,b".split(",") → ["a", "b"]
words() / lines()Splits on spaces / on line breaks"to be or".words() → ["to", "be", "or"]
replace(old, new)Replaces every match"banana".replace("a", "o") → "bonono"
contains(x) / starts(x) / ends(x)Yes or no questions about the text"hello".starts("he") → yes
count(x)How many times x appears"banana".count("a") → 3
index(x)Position of x (counting from 0), or none"hello".index("l") → 2
length()Number of characters"hello".length() → 5
reverse()Backwards"abc".reverse() → "cba"
repeat(n)Repeats the text n times"ab".repeat(3) → "ababab"
pad(width, fill) / padend(width, fill)Pads on the left / right (fill defaults to a space)"7".pad(3, "0") → "007"
chars()One item per character"hi".chars() → ["h", "i"]

Voice Guide

Tap the mic and talk. Your words appear in the editor as you speak, using your browser's built-in speech recognition. A few words work as voice commands and type something other than themselves. Typing by hand always works normally.

Good to know

run, line, pair, par, curl, index, back, it, dot, quote, the operator words, and spoken numbers are reserved while dictating. If a variable of yours has one of those names, it gets swallowed as the command, so type that name by hand or wrap it in quote ... quote.

par and pair sound alike in fast speech but are deliberately not cross-corrected: they are the open and close.

The the <request> AI helper is switched off in this build. The corrections above cover the words Vertex's own commands collide with; if the browser keeps mishearing a keyword such as define, type it.
